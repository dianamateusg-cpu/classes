The verb to be

One verb, three forms in the present, and it appears in almost every sentence you will make this week.

The forms

I am
yo soy / estoy
you are
tú eres / estás
he, she, it is
él, ella es / está
we are
nosotros somos / estamos
they are
ellos son / están

Making questions

To ask a question, put the verb first:

You are from Bogotá. → Are you from Bogotá?

This is the whole rule. There is no extra word to add, unlike most other English verbs.
