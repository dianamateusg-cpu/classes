Greetings and introductions

The words we will use in class today. Read them first, then try the practice at the bottom.

Words to know

to introduce
presentar
a greeting
un saludo
Nice to meet you.
Mucho gusto.
How do you spell it?
¿Cómo se escribe?
Where are you from?
¿De dónde eres?
a surname
un apellido

In conversation

— Hi, I’m Ana. Nice to meet you.
— Nice to meet you too. Where are you from?

Notice that the second speaker adds too. In English we rarely repeat the whole phrase — we shorten it and add too or as well.

Practice

  1. Introduce yourself to the person next to you.
  2. Ask them where they are from, and spell your surname out loud.
  3. Swap roles and do it again, faster.
Homework Write six sentences introducing someone in your family. Bring them to the next class.
